# elite-shared

Shared files for all Elite apps: `HANDOVER.md` (the shared memory), `design/` (brand rules, tokens, logos) and `docs/` (designs).

## Before you start

1. `git pull`.
2. Read `HANDOVER.md` in full, then `design/elite-design.md` for any UI work.

## Rules

* `HANDOVER.md` is the single record: change the existing line rather than adding a competing one. Where it disagrees with a repo, the repo wins; fix the note.
* Follow HANDOVER.md section 8 (secrets, deleting data, style).
* Brand PDFs and `design/` are the source of truth for every app. Do not invent new styles.
* Documents for Greg: short and structured, avoid hyphens, no sign offs.

## When you finish

Update `HANDOVER.md` (Done, To do, Infrastructure, Open, change log) and push.
